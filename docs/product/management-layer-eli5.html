ELI5 · Historical product explanation

Managing ten thousand computers

What is the proposed management layer? When an app gives each of ten thousand users an AI helper, manage those ten thousand computers for the app owner.

Orange: Cloudflare, the computer factory Green: nimplex, the manager

Page 1

Two kinds of customers

On the left are engineers who want to build agents themselves. Cloudflare serves them with components and computers. On the right are product builders who want agents inside their apps without constructing the underlying infrastructure. This proposal targets the second group.

Page 2

The challenge is ten thousand computers

Adding agents to an app with ten thousand users creates ten thousand helpers spending the owner's money and holding user credentials . Who watches, contains failures, and stops overspending?

Page 3

Three responsibilities

Each workspace has our manager checking spending, credentials, and tool access before work proceeds.

Page 4

Stop spending during execution

A request-entry check resembles a guard at the entrance who can reject arrival but cannot control continuing work inside. Our proposed manager operates inside the workspace and, when the budget reaches its cap, stops execution immediately , even during an unfinished task.

Page 5

A few lines for the customer

Product builders install our SDK and connect a button; the platform handles the execution path.

Final page

Product statement

Cloudflare rents computers to engineers ；
nimplex manages ten thousand workspaces for a product's ten thousand users ——
Each has metering, credential boundaries, and permissions.
The historical positioning separates infrastructure builders from product operators. Its claimed market gap was a hypothesis; later nimplex decisions supersede this presentation.
